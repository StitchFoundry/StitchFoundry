# Free starter pack

Twelve original designs: SVG art, finished stitch files (DST and PES) and a preview PNG each.
All art was drawn by hand as plain SVG shapes for StitchFoundry (no AI, no traced or third-party
art, no brands or characters). The stitch files and previews are made from the SVGs by `build.sh`
with `stitchfoundry-cli` (Convert Artwork to Embroidery, default settings). They are 80 mm wide at
most, so they fit a 4x4 hoop. Pre-release: not yet test-sewn on every machine.
